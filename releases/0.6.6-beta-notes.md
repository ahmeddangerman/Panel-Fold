- Remember the switch and strength across comics and app restarts, and include both in settings backups.
- Dim the displayed artwork uniformly through panning, zooming, page turns and detail-tile updates. The dimmer adds a final drawing pass without altering source images, inverting colors, allocating new page bitmaps, or rebuilding the detail cache.
- Keep the existing reader UI, controls and black margins at their existing appearance.

Open a comic, tap to show the reader controls, then select **Options → Page dimmer**. Enable the switch and adjust the slider; turning the switch off restores the original page brightness while retaining the chosen strength.
