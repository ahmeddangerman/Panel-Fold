- Delete a custom collection from the Collections screen after a confirmation. This removes that grouping and its comic assignments, including assignments waiting in a restored backup; comic files, library entries, progress, and bookmarks stay.
- Follow each comic's saved reading direction in the reader progress line and seek slider, and in the Home and Library progress bars. Manga set to RTL now advances visually from right to left.
- Press and hold a page to open a temporary 2× circular magnifier. Drag the held finger to inspect another area; the lens uses the reader's original-image detail tiles and disappears when the finger lifts.

Verification: all 150 JVM tests pass. The Android test APK compiles, including tests for collection deletion and magnifier press/hold/release. Debug lint reports no errors, seven existing warnings, and one informational item. The Android interaction tests were not run on a physical tablet or emulator in this workspace.
